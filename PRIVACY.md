# Privacy Policy — SkipKey

**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Summary

SkipKey does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What SkipKey stores

| Data | Where | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| Your chosen hotkey (key code + modifiers) | `chrome.storage.sync` | So your setting follows you across devices |
| Toast and auto-skip preferences | `chrome.storage.sync` | Your configuration choices |
| Skip counter | `chrome.storage.local` | Shows how many ads you've skipped (never transmitted) |

## What SkipKey does NOT do

- ❌ No analytics or telemetry
- ❌ No network requests of any kind
- ❌ No browsing history collection
- ❌ No cookies or tracking pixels
- ❌ No data shared with third parties
- ❌ No remote code execution
- ❌ No data sold or monetized

## Permissions explained

| Permission | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| `storage` | Save your hotkey preference |
| Host access to `youtube.com` | The content script needs to find and click the Skip button on the page you're watching |

## Open source

The complete source code is available at [github.com/WinterJackson/ski](https://github.com/WinterJackson/ski). You can read every line to verify these claims.
